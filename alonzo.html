Sensei Alonzo Henderson

6th Dan OLY • Founder of Cabra Judo Club • Chair of IJA Kata Commission

Started judo at 5 years of age. Over 60 years practicing judo makes Alonzo Henderson the longest practicing judoka in the history of the Irish Judo Association. An Olympian at the 1980 Moscow Olympic Games, World Championship finalist, 26-time Senior All-Ireland Champion, Coach Tutor, and the bedrock of martial arts development in Ireland.

🇯🇵 World Champion Tribute • Tokai University (Feb 2023)

"I Never Forgot the Impact of His Powerful Grip"

Personal recollections from Kiyoto Katsuki, 1979 World Judo Champion (Paris, -71kg)

“

"First of all, I would like to send my congratulations to Mr. Alonzo Henderson's promotion for 6th dan.

I fought him at the 1979 World Judo Championships in Paris. Honestly, I didn't check Mr. Henderson. I thought my rivals were Mr. Ezio Gamba of Italy and Mr. Neil Adams of the U.K. But I soon realized my expectation was completely wrong when we grabbed each other. I never forgot the impact of his powerful grip. Mr. Henderson was big, I guess he was 180cm tall, and a tough Judoka. Fortunately, I won the match, but I couldn't do what I wanted to do for the first 2 minutes at all.

Mr. Henderson is one of several unforgettable players in my Judo career. I can still remember every single moment of our fight.

After the championship, a Japanese TV station gave me the video. I sometimes watch it and think about my opponents, how they are doing.

When I know Mr. Henderson's promotion to the 6th dan, my heart became so warm. We haven't met for more than 40 years after the championship, but I can vividly imagine how much he has dedicated himself to Judo and his home country.

I really wanted to visit you in Ireland to shake your big hands. Instead, I say congratulations from the bottom of my heart. I hope we can meet again in the future to celebrate our lives."

🥋
Kiyoto Katsuki (香月 清人)
1979 World Judo Champion (-71kg, Paris) • First World Champion from Tokai University • Osaka Police
✉️ Communicated via Teruyoshi Yamaguchi, Tokai University Judo Club (Feb 4, 2023) on the occasion of Sensei Alonzo's 6th Dan award.

National Dominance: Junior & Senior Titles

Unprecedented All-Ireland reign spanning four consecutive decades

1964 – 1971: All Ireland Junior Champion (IJA)

Alonzo held various All-Ireland Judo championship titles and All-Ireland Schoolboy championships in multiple weight categories over a period of 8 dominant years.

The Historic Triple Sweep: In a single year as a junior, Alonzo won both the under 70kg and the open category across the Under 15s, Under 17s, AND Under 19s in the Irish Schoolboy Championships — a record that has never been matched.

1973 – 2002: All Ireland Senior Champion (IJA)

Over a legendary 29-year career at the pinnacle of Irish senior judo, Alonzo won a total of 26 Senior All-Ireland Championships across 5 different weight categories as well as the Open division.

Record-Breaking Firsts in Irish Judo History

Pioneering milestones recorded by the Irish Judo Association

  • 🏆
    Double Champion in One Year: The first person in the IJA to win both a Junior and a Senior All-Ireland championship in the same year.
  • ⭐
    Youngest Senior Champion: The youngest person in IJA history to win a Senior All-Ireland Championship.
  • 🥋
    Youngest Inter-Provincial Senior: The youngest player to represent Leinster Seniors at the Inter-Provincial Championships.
  • 🌍
    Youngest Senior International (16 Years): The youngest person to represent the IJA at senior international level.
  • 🎖️
    Youngest 1st Dan by Bat Sagun (16 Years): The youngest person in the IJA to achieve 1st Dan by Bat Sagun (Bat-to-Sagari line-up / 5½ wins).
  • 🎖️
    Youngest 2nd Dan by Bat Sagun (18 Years): The youngest person in the IJA to achieve 2nd Dan by Bat Sagun (5½ wins).
  • 🎖️
    Youngest 3rd Dan by Bat Sagun (20 Years): The youngest person in the IJA to achieve 3rd Dan by Bat Sagun (5½ wins).
  • 🥋
    First Competitive 4th Dan (25 Years): The first competitive 4th Dan in the history of the IJA.
  • 🥋
    First Competitive 5th Dan (29 Years): The first competitive 5th Dan in the history of the IJA.
  • 📜
    Exemplary Kata Examination (85% Pass): First judoka in Ireland examined on Nage no kata, Katame no kata, and Go no sen kata with an average score pass of 85% for 4th and 5th Dan.

Kata Commission Leadership & Coaching Pedigree

Education, Kata mastery, and mentoring four generations of Irish judoka

Sensei Alonzo has been actively coaching for 49 years across all levels — from early junior grassroots to senior national squads and international competitors. He has instructed at prestigious summer camps across Ireland and Europe, coaching both individuals and teams competing on the global stage.

Current Roles & Professional Credentials:

  • Chair of the IJA Kata Commission: Directing kata standards, technical examinations, and educational clinics nationwide.
  • Coach Tutor: Accredited to train and certify coaches for the national governing body.
  • Level 2 Coach: Certified by Sport Ireland and the Irish Judo Association.

Clubs & Universities Mentored by Alonzo Sensei:

Throughout 49 years of coaching, Alonzo has founded, led, or coached at Ireland's foremost universities, collegiate teams, and community dojos:
