Safeguarding & Club Policies

Our highest priority: child protection, data privacy, and ethical standards across all club operations.

Ensuring that your children are safe and well cared for at all times is a key concern for our club

If you have any concerns about children (your own or others) and their interaction with coaches, adults, or children in the club, you should contact our Children's Officer Mary immediately. Mary will ensure that your concerns are taken on board and acted upon independently from the coaching staff and in full confidence with the support of Sport Ireland and other statutory agencies when required.

Child Welfare & Protection

Children's Officer: Mary McDonald

Mary is the go-to person for any matters involving children's welfare and safety in the club.

💬 Contact: 086 150 2011 ✉️ cabrajudo@gmail.com

🛡️ Garda Vetted Coaches

All coaches, assistants, and adult volunteers at Cabra Judo Club undergo mandatory National Vetting Bureau procedures and child protection training before working with juniors.

🤝 Independent Reporting

Our Children's Officer operates independently of the coaching roster. Any matter brought forward is treated with strict confidentiality and guided by Sport Ireland's Code of Ethics.

📜 Respect & Inclusion

We maintain zero tolerance for bullying, harassment, or discrimination. Judo's fundamental principles teach mutual benefit (Jita Kyoei) and maximum efficiency with minimal effort (Seiryoku Zenyo).
